# Legal & Compliance Notice

This project is a **personal knowledge-management toolkit**. It is not a piracy, mass-scraping, or redistribution service.

## Allowed use

- Download or process videos **only** when you have a legitimate right to view them (e.g. content you can legally watch on the platform under your account / public access rules).
- Use outputs for **personal study and note-taking**.

## Prohibited use

- Mass piracy, bulk unauthorized downloading, scraping-for-redistribution, or commercial re-upload of others’ media.
- Committing **copyrighted transcripts, comments, or media** to public repositories.
- Bypassing paywalls, DRM, or access controls beyond what the platform exposes to a normal logged-in viewer with cookies you own.

## Content ownership

- Video audio/video, **transcripts**, and **comment text** belong to the original creators and/or the platform.
- Treat generated literature-card drafts that embed that text as **private working notes** unless you have clear rights to share them.
- Documentation examples must use **anonymized** comment samples (e.g. `@用户A`), never real usernames or personal data.

## Technical disclaimer (WBI / anti-bot)

- Bilibili **WBI** request signing used in some scripts is a **reverse-engineered** compatibility layer.
- It may **break without notice** when the platform changes APIs or anti-bot rules.
- Rate-limit / 412 handling is best-effort only; aggressive retry loops can worsen blocks. Prefer slow, sparse requests.

## Cookies & accounts

- Prefer an **anonymous** or secondary account cookie file (`scripts/jar.txt`).
- Never commit `SESSDATA`, full browser exports, or other secrets. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
